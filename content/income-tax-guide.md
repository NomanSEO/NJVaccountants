This guide explains the general process for preparing and filing an income tax return through Pakistan's FBR IRIS portal. Filing requirements, tax treatment and deadlines depend on the taxpayer's circumstances. Check the selected tax year and the latest FBR notices before submitting.

## Choose the correct tax year

For a person using the normal June year-end, **Tax Year 2026 covers 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026**. Tax Year 2025 covered 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025. A special tax year may follow a different period. Select the correct period in IRIS; do not use an older year's records or rates simply because an earlier guide mentions them.

## Check whether you must file

Filing obligations are set out in [section 114 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/Categ/Income-Tax-Ordinance/326). They can arise from income, business activity, registration and other conditions. Do not assume that income below a salary tax threshold automatically removes a filing obligation. An individual, association of persons and company can have different forms and due dates. Seek advice if your status is unclear.

## Gather the records you need

- CNIC or registration details and access to your IRIS account.
- Salary certificate, business accounts or other income records relevant to the selected tax year.
- Bank statements, marketplace reports, payment gateway records, courier settlements and COD collection records where relevant.
- Expense, inventory, refund and return records for business income calculations.
- Withholding tax certificates, computerized payment receipts and advance tax evidence.
- Asset, liability, receipt and personal expense details needed for a wealth statement where applicable.

For an online seller, reconcile orders, returns, courier deductions, payment gateway settlements and bank receipts before using these figures in a return. Sales deposits alone do not establish taxable profit.

## File through FBR IRIS

1. Use the official [IRIS portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/). First-time users should follow FBR's registration instructions. Existing registered users may need to recover or enroll their login credentials.
2. Open the income tax return or declaration for the relevant tax year and taxpayer type. IRIS labels and forms can change; use [FBR's current filing instructions](https://fbr.gov.pk/categ/file-income-tax-return/51147/80860/71160) if the menu differs.
3. Enter the relevant income, allowable business expenses, tax deducted or paid, and other required information. Review imported withholding data against your own evidence.
4. Complete and reconcile the wealth statement if required for your return. FBR explains that the current and previous wealth movement must reconcile with receipts and expenses for submission.
5. Review the calculation and declarations, pay any amount due using an FBR payment channel, verify and submit. Confirm that the return and wealth statement have moved from **Draft** to **Completed Task** in IRIS. Save your records and acknowledgement.

FBR's [IRIS help centre](https://e.fbr.gov.pk/SOP/IRIS/help/index.html) includes return, registration and wealth statement guides.

## Tax Year 2026 rates: use the schedule for your income type

The salaried individual schedule for Tax Year 2026, explained in [FBR Circular No. 01 of 2025–26](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2025841183918948CircularNo01of2025-26IncomeTax.pdf), is:

| Annual taxable salary | Tax calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Up to PKR 600,000 | Nil |
| Above PKR 600,000 to PKR 1,200,000 | 1% of the amount above PKR 600,000 |
| Above PKR 1,200,000 to PKR 2,200,000 | PKR 6,000 plus 11% of the amount above PKR 1,200,000 |
| Above PKR 2,200,000 to PKR 3,200,000 | PKR 116,000 plus 23% of the amount above PKR 2,200,000 |
| Above PKR 3,200,000 to PKR 4,100,000 | PKR 346,000 plus 30% of the amount above PKR 3,200,000 |
| Above PKR 4,100,000 | PKR 616,000 plus 35% of the amount above PKR 4,100,000 |

These are **salary rates for Tax Year 2026**, not a universal table for e-commerce business profits. Additional provisions, including a high-income surcharge where applicable, may affect the final calculation. The treatment of business income, final or minimum tax provisions, sales tax and other taxes must be assessed separately. Check the [Income Tax Ordinance and current FBR publications](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/Categ/Income-Tax-Ordinance/326) for your facts. Withholding on marketplace, courier, banking or other transactions also depends on the law and the transaction; compare deductions with FBR's [Tax Year 2026 withholding rate card](https://fbr.gov.pk/withholding-taxes-rate-card/174298/174301).

## Filing deadline and late filing

For persons whose Tax Year 2026 return was originally due on **30 September 2026**, [FBR Circular No. 3 of 2026–27](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026930229176620Extension.pdf) extended that deadline to **15 October 2026**. The circular applies to the persons described in it; it does not set every company's deadline. Check for later FBR notices before relying on this date.

Late filing can have consequences under the [Income Tax Ordinance, including section 182](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/section-182/152727), and may affect Active Taxpayer List status. Penalties depend on the applicable provision and circumstances; a blanket “PKR 1,000 per day” claim is unreliable. FBR [explains how to file after a deadline and revise a return](https://fbr.gov.pk/categ/file-income-tax-return/51147/80860/71160). If you discover an error, check the revision rules before resubmitting.

## Before you submit

Check the tax year, taxpayer type, income classification, supporting records, withholding credits, wealth reconciliation and payment details. Keep copies of the filed return and supporting documents. For an e-commerce business with complex channels or returns, professional review can help identify gaps before filing.

*Reviewed against FBR guidance and the Tax Year 2026 extension circular on 13 October 2026. This is general information, not a determination of any person's tax liability.*
