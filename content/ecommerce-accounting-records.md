Good e-commerce accounting starts with records that explain what happened between an order and the money received. Online sellers in Pakistan often work across websites, marketplaces, social platforms, couriers, payment gateways and banks. Each source records a different part of the transaction.

## Keep a complete sales trail

Retain order exports with order IDs, dates, product details, selling prices, discounts, delivery charges, payment methods and order status. Keep records of cancellations, returns, exchanges and refunds. Sales placed and sales finally settled can differ, so record the timing and reason for each difference.

## Reconcile collections and settlements

Keep courier and payment gateway statements showing collections, fees, deductions and settlement dates. Match each settlement to orders and bank receipts. A useful monthly review lists outstanding collections, disputed deductions and amounts received after month-end.

## Record costs and expenses

Maintain purchase invoices, inventory movement records, packaging costs, courier charges, marketing spend, platform fees, payroll and other operating expenses. Allocate costs consistently enough to understand product and channel performance. Separate business transactions from an owner's personal spending.

## Prepare useful monthly reporting

Once transactions are reconciled, review revenue, gross margin, operating expenses, cash balances, outstanding settlements and stock. Management accounts are most useful when owners can trace reported figures back to source records and investigate exceptions.

Retention and tax reporting requirements depend on the business and applicable law. Discuss the records and reporting scope with your accountant when setting up a process.
